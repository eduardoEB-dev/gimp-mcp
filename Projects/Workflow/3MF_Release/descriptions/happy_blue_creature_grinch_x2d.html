Happy Blue Creature Grinch Cooler Cup (X2D)

A mini sideline-cooler style can holder for a standard 12 oz can, with the happy blue creature in a green Grinch suit, Santa hat and red bow on the front, and red ribs on the grips.

Includes two caps, a handle, spigot and pins. Uses 9 filaments, so it is set up for the X2D with multiple AMS units. The cup plate has a pause at 89 mm to switch blue for yellow.

Plates: cup, lid, handle, spigot + pins, cap.
