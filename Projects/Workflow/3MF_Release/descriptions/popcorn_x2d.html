Cat in the Hat Popcorn Bucket (X2D)

A one-piece, hat-shaped popcorn bucket with wavy red and white stripes and a wide white brim, printed in two colors (red and white PLA).

Sized and set up for the Bambu Lab X2D (0.4 nozzle, 0.16mm High Quality). The stripes are painted on a single body, so it prints in one go with no assembly.

Plates: bucket (one piece).
