Little Yellow Bird Cooler Cup

A mini sideline-cooler style can holder for a standard 12 oz can, in light blue with a yellow bird outlined in black on the front and the bird's face on the yellow lid. Black carry handle, pins and spigot.

Plates: cooler cup; lid & pins; handle & spigot.
