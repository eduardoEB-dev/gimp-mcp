Beagle Dog Bowl Candle Holder (A1 mini)

A sitting cartoon beagle that holds a jar candle in the dog bowl on its head. White head and body, black ears, nose, eyes, smile and back spot, red bowl and collar, white paw emblem on the bowl. An optional green grass base is included.

Set up for the Bambu Lab A1 mini (0.4 nozzle, 0.20mm Standard, PLA). Plates 1 and 5 use matte PLA for the white parts. The bowl pocket is 106mm across, for a jar candle about 4 in (102mm) wide. Printed in parts and assembled with glue: the included glue keys join the body, collar, head, ears and nose, and the dowel joins the head to the bowl. Supports are enabled on the body, head and collar.

Plates: 1 body and paw emblem (white); 2 bowl (red); 3 collar (red); 4 ears, nose and back spot (black); 5 head (white with black eyes and smile), dowel and glue keys; 6 optional grass base (green).

The grass base has a pocket for the figure. Glue the body into it; left unglued, the base adds no protection against tipping.

Candle safety: use a jar candle only, keep the figure on a stable surface, and never leave a burning candle unattended.
