Cat in the Hat Hot Air Balloon (A1 mini)

A striped-hat hot air balloon floating over a cloud, with a red couch as the basket and a gold control panel of wheel, levers, pedals and knobs. Printed in PLA: red, white, gold, silver and dark brown.

The hat is split into four stacked stripe rings, a top cap and a separate flat brim, so each piece prints in a single color. The clouds come in three parts (base, mid, top). The balloon ropes are not printed: use lengths of gold filament. The small controls are set up for a 0.2 nozzle; everything else uses the A1 mini 0.4 nozzle at 0.16mm High Quality.

Plates: 4 hat rings (red/white), top cap, brim, couch, bracket, base cloud, mid + top cloud, controls (gold/silver, 0.2 nozzle), dark knobs (0.2 nozzle).
