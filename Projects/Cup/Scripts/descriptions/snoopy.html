Snoopy Grinch Cooler Cup

A mini sideline-cooler style can holder for a standard 12 oz can, featuring Snoopy in a Grinch suit with red and green candy-cane ribs.

Two front designs: "Merry Christmas" and "Merry X-Mas", each in a standard and a 5-color version. Red cap with a black paw print.

Plates: both cup designs, paw cap + pins + spigot, handle.
