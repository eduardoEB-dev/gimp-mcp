Grinch Cooler Cup

A mini sideline-cooler style can holder for a standard 12 oz can, with a multicolor "Santa Hates You" Grinch on the front and red ribs on the grips.

Includes two swappable caps: "Merry Grinchmas" and "You're a Mean One, Mr. Grinch".

Plates: cup, Merry Grinchmas cap, Mean One cap, handle + spigot, pins.
