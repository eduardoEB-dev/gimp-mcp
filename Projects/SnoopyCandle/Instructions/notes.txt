Assembly notes (user, 2026-11-03, plus Project.md and 3MF description)
Grass base is optional. It helps with tipping. Print it to be safe. Must be glued or adds no protection.
Bowl pocket 106 mm across, for jar candle about 4 in (102 mm) wide, 12 mm deep.
Plates 1 and 5 use matte PLA. 6 plates. 0.4 nozzle.
Glue keys: 4 collar, 6 ear and nose. 5.8 mm tall collar keys, 6.2 mm tall ear and nose keys.
V4 update (user, 2026-11-04): head no longer printed with smile and eyes. Eyes and smile print separately and are glued in.
Eyes are straightforward to add to the head.
Smile: start on one end. Glue the T end. Wait for that to dry. Slowly warm the smile with a lighter or a heat gun to make it softer. Then trace along the groove with the smile and glue it as you go.
Everything else is the same.
